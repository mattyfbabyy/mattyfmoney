Privacy

Draft pending compliance review. Any privacy language the firm requires will replace or add to this page.

Who runs this site

This site is run by Matt Friedman in Chicago, Illinois.

What this site collects

The only information this site asks for is what you enter when you book a meeting: your name, your email, your phone number, and anything you choose to share about why you're booking.

This site does not run advertising trackers or analytics.

How it's used

To schedule your meeting, prepare for it, and follow up with you afterward. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone for their marketing.

Services this site relies on

Bookings are handled by a scheduling service, which stores what you enter under its own privacy policy. The site is hosted by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what you've shared at any time. Contact details will be listed here before this site is made public.
