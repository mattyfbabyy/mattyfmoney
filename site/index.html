Before you book

The questions people ask me most.

What actually happens at the first meeting?

Mostly, I listen. We talk about why you booked, what you'd change about your money, and your goals for the next few years. I'll share how we work. If you see value, we set up the next conversation. That's it.

Are you going to try to sell me something?

Not at the first meeting. There's nothing to buy and nothing to sign. Later on, if a product genuinely fits a goal you've told me about, I'll show you why and you decide.

Is it really free? How do you get paid?

The meeting is free. If you decide to put coverage in place later, I'm paid by the insurance company, not by a check from you. I'll explain exactly how that works before you decide anything.

I already have a financial advisor.

Great, I'm happy to work alongside them. Nothing has to move.

I have coverage through work. Isn't that enough?

Often not. Work coverage is frequently a small multiple of salary and typically ends when the job does. It's a nice benefit, not a plan.

I don't have much to work with yet.

That's when the order matters most. We plan a few years at a time, so you start where you are, not where someone thinks you should be.

Do I need to bring anything?

No. Bring your questions and an honest answer to the snap-your-fingers question. If we keep going, I'll tell you what's useful for next time.

Will you pressure me?

No. If the plan is right, it'll still be right next week. I spent ten years in real estate watching people get pushed into decisions. I don't do that.
